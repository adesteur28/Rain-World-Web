*☆ Slugcat ☆*

♡ The Slimey slugcat ♡

Overview:

Rivulet is an adorable and capable fellow, slipping through the map with ease. Their speed is essential to their campaign- however the fast pace of this slugcat is necessary to its survival!

Speed - strength - food

Diet- normal. 5 pips are needed to hibernate, but only one can be stored. Plants, small prey. (additional information: rivulet can pop bubblefruit by holding them.) However, rivulet has the HIGHEST movement speed, jump height, and swim speed of ANY slugcat. They have a slide that takes up about half the screen. Their backflips can get them into higher spaces, skipping lots of parkour. Their speed means that they can actually bypass a lot of predators, including things like monster kelp and pole plants. Their slimy and fast nature also helps them climb the ropes of proto long legs.

Special mechanics:

Swimming:

Rivulet resembles an axolotl aesthetically, and whether it be due to the rainfall or the semi-aquatic nature of thai slugcat, they can eat underwater, have a high lung capacity, swim faster, etc. This allows them to reach some portions of the map that require you to swim underwater for extended periods of time. Additionally, the rain is a lot less dangerous to rivulet. This is necessary in their rainy world. (Also, pressing down while jumping and about to enter water lets you "ploink", so shoot out at an angle.)

Rain:

The speed of this slugcat is necessary as the rain is much more frequent. The pips on the screen are each 30 seconds, and a timer until the rain. While most slugcats have anywhere from 6.5-13 minutes per cycle, the average rivulet cycle can go as low as 2.5-4 minutes in early game. This may seem like enough but it is difficult when taking into account the hunt for food, dodging monsters, and other issues that persist with the ecosystem.

Shelter Failures:

While regularly the chance is 2%, and can only happen later for some slugcats, for rivulet base cycle failure chance is 25%. This changes to 40% in certain areas. When a Shelter failure occurs, the Shelter Slugcat wakes up in is open and partially flooded, and it is raining outside; this is also known as a precycle. A flooded Shelter cannot be used again until Slugcat has hibernated in a different Shelter. Shelters with entrances at the bottom do not flood during precycles and can be hibernated in again immediately. Precycle rain is lighter than end-of-cycle rain, is non-lethal, and dissipates after 2-5 minutes. The rain pushes creatures and objects against the ground until it lets up. The rain periodically slows, allowing for movement in roofless areas. The cycle timer is paused until the rain has fully stopped, making precycles longer than normal cycles.

Backstory:

Due to the collapse of Fivepebbles structure, it is safe to assume that the world rivulet will inhabit in the future compared to survivors. Five pebbles mentions having seen other slugcats, implying a shared universe. While we don't get much of a personal story from rivulet, she progresses the story in a crucial way.

Campaign goals/winning:

As always, ascension is a way to beat the game. However, the intended journey I would say is a much more personally rewarding ending. When you meet Five Pebbles, he asks you to take the infraction pearl- a gravity altering powersource, and give it to Looks To The Moon. He shows remorse for what he has done, and upon delivering this to Looks To The Moon she is concerned of its origins- however, she asks for Rivulet to replace it, and bringing it to the heart restores some of the functions of Looks To The Moon's superstructure. Via dialogue, we get the (rather adorable) knowledge that Looks To The Moon has named rivulet Ruffles- and the ending portrait confirms that rivulet chooses to stay by Looks To The Moon's side.
