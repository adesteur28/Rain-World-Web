*☆ Gourmand ☆*

♡ The Chunky Slugcat ♡

Overview:

Due to being a chubbier and larger slugcat, there are a few special talents this slugcat has! It may require more food, and tire easily, but it has many unique and beneficial mechanics to make up for its large mass. The gourmand is a rather content slugcat, with food on its mind near constantly. It is a friendly and resourceful creature, who cares for others.

Speed - strength - food

This slugcat is a bit slower than other slugcats, yet stronger. his throw strength is almost double other playable slugcats, however he is easily tired. After throwing a spear, his 2nd throw is half survivor strength. He then needs time to revover. This also happens after excess jumping and manouvering. While tired he will move at half his walk speed, his strength is impacted, and his jumps are impacted too. 7 food pips are required to hibernate, and 4 are avalible for storage. They are omnivourous: they can eat slain creatures but only for half the canivourous food pips.

Special mechanics:

Offensive:

This slugcat has almost double survivor strength in its first spear throw, and half in its 2nd. It also can roll for long periods of time without tiring. Additionally, there is a fun move that only this slugcat can do due to its size and weight: death drops! When on a higher surface, gourmand can jump/fall onto the head of an enemy to deal damage and stun the enemy. In some cases, this can instant kill too! It also has a longer slide with temporary immunity, stun, and .25 damage.

Food:

When two different foods are held in each hand, and you hold UP and GRAB, you can create a meal. This grants you an additional food pip!

Regurgitating:

If Gourmand has no stored stomach item, holding Grab while standing still causes them to regurgitate a random item from the following list at the cost of 1 food pip. Attempting this without any food pips briefly stuns Gourmand, and no item spawns.

Crafting:

Crafting is done by pressing UP and GRAB at the same time while standing still. Combining two objects produces a new object. A crafting menu is available on the rain world wiki, and it is rather extensive! There are so many combinations- yes you can craft living things and even singularity bombs…

Backstory:

Gourmand is a character with no confirmable backstory, however he does appear to be the most content and caring of the slugcats, starting a school for others and being content to live out his days with food.

Campaign goals/winning:

There are many ways to win in the gourmand campaign. For one, the food quest: this a very unique ending to this slugcat only. By collecting 22 various foods and consuming them from various areas of the map, you unlock an alternate ending and slugpups. You travel far and wide, meeting a group of slugcats> The ending art shows gourmand feasting, sleeping with, and teaching the other slugcats and slugpups (baby slugcats) about their world. This area is then unlocked for other slugcats as well to visit- (survivor and monk are shown to reunite here… that lowk made me cry.) Additionally, you can also ascend in the depth as the standard ending.
