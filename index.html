What this page is for:

This page is here to talk about all of the different slugcats. This overview goes over the basic game slugcats (Monk, survivor, and hunter.) It also goes over the downpour DLC slugcats (Gourmand, Artificer, Rivulet, Spearmaster, Saint, INV). I will give an overview, speed and strength, special mechanics, and general backstory of each of the characters. Also, I will go into the campaigns- their goals, endings, etc.
