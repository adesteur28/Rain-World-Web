*☆ Slugcat ☆*

♡ The Base slugcat ♡

Overview:

Survivor is the game base slugcat. It is the original campaign, and one could say the main character. Before the DLC, it served as the standard playthrough for all players.

Speed - strength - food

This cat is the base stats compared to other cats. Diet "normal". 4 pips are needed to hibernate, and 3 can be stored. They eat plants and small prey.

Special mechanics:

This slugcat is the base for all others. Therefore, there ARE no special mechanics!

Backstory:

Survivor appeared to be in a family- (Monk being their sibling.) As they travel on a log, the rain comes early, and Survivor falls into the outskirts. The art is gorgeous, and tragic. Then begins their campaign.

Campaign goals/winning:

Typically, there is only one way to beat the game: ascension. After talking to Five Pebbles, he will send you to the depths. There, you will travel, your screen distorting more and more, until you reach the void. After a few minutes of swimming, you reach ascension. You swim along all your cycles and finish the game. All slugcats are capable of ascension, and it is the main ending for all slugcats. However, if you have finished the food quest and outer expanse ending in gourmand, you will unlock that ending for survivors as well. It is a peaceful ending, as survivors wait for a community to form, alone under the orb.
