In order to play as saint, you must finish both rivulet and spearmasters campaigns.

Speed - strength - food

4 pips are required to hibernate as saints, and only one can be stored. Saint has a strictly vegetarian pacifist diet- the only exception being eggbug eggs. Eating anything else results in a stun and reset of progress, making its diet challenging to complete.

Special mechanics:

Freezing:

Saint has no rain during their campaign, however they have a unique temperature mechanic. They have pips of warmth, and any outdoor area will drain their warmth. There are few heat producing items to help the saint, and the most effective one being a lantern. It becomes colder, and your health reduces more and more the longer the cycle goes on for. When the cycle is up, it will turn into a blizzard as opposed to rain.

Grapple tongue:

Grapple tongue. Need I say more? It functions similarly to a grapple worm, however it is available 24/7, much more mobile and versatile, takes no inventory slot, and remains sticking to a location even upon character death. The tongue can be used to speedily swing yourself through the map, avoid predators,and more.
