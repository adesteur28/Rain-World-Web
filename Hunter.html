*☆ Slugcat ☆*

♡ The Carnivorous slugcat ♡

Overview:

Hunter is stronger and faster, but this serves as the game's hard mode for a reason. Between the cycle limit, and the carnivorous diet of the hunter, this slugcat serves as the base game's "hard mode". It is unlocked after the completion of either the survivor, or monk campaigns in the base game. It also is a very unique campaign, with a unique count down system that I will mention later, and a permadeath that can affect other campaigns.

Speed - strength - food

6 food pips are required to hibernate, while 3 can be stored for the next cycle. But here's the twist: they are carnivorous. Plants and batflies only fill ¼ a food pip. Hunter can eat slain creature corpses. They do have the advantage of being able to store a spear on their back, which no other slugcats can do! They are about double skilled, and generally stronger, faster, and more capable than survivor. However, creature reputation is lower, making creatures more inclined to aggression towards hunters.

Special mechanics:

Already said, but hunter is a carnivorous slugcat who is capable of holding 2 spears at once, by putting one on their back. However, the main special mechanic of this slugcat is their permadeath and cycle count. Unlike all other slugcats, where the cycles count up every time you hibernate, for hunter it is the opposite. You start on cycle 19 and count DOWN. Once you reach zero, you begin to go into the negatives. You are still capable of hibernation and saved progress in the negatives, but there are two notable changes. Gameplay wise, hunter will have spasms and seizures. Hunter becomes more unreliable, shaky, and this makes the game that much harder. Additionally, if you were to die in the negative cycles you would be put into permadeath. While normally dying means rebirth, and lost karma, once your 19 cycles are up you simply have no rebirths in this game. This paired with creature aggression, karma struggles, and more is what makes this character so difficult.

Backstory:

There is a special scenario to see "Hunter Long Legs". If you play hunter and unlock permadeath (depending on the method of death), then in certain areas of the gourmand campaign you can actually encounter a rot entity labeled Hunter Long Legs. It is a rot entity- stronger, more sensitive to sound, yet slower. However, the X and spot on hunter has grown to become rot, implying that the illness is what caused hunter to count down from 19 cycles, and become shaky and dizzy in the negatives. It is the only time we see character death or a permadeath too, and echos are needed to ascend as hunter.

Campaign goals/winning:

There are 2 endings, however also a mini-mission included. The permadeath ending is obviously the easiest to obtain, however the ascension is a much more difficult ending to achieve. Due to the limited cycles, you must limit your deaths in order to maintain the karma required to make it through the karma gates- typically echos help fill in the gaps. However, hunter starts the game with a green neuron from another iterator. This can be delivered to Looks To The Moon, to revive her.
