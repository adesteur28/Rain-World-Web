*☆ Slugcat ☆*

♡ The Spikey slugcat ♡

Overview:

Spearmaster is an abnormality among slugcats. Its spikey tail with seemingly infinite spears, its lack of mouth: it was a slugcat created by an iterator and not naturally born. The lore significance of this character is irrefutable.

Speed - strength - food

5 food pips are required to hibernate, and 5 can be saved until the next cycle. They cannot feed on dead creatures, however this character is unique. They have no mouth. Instead, they must feed with the spear-like needles they create from their spikey tail. Spearmaster can hold a spear in each hand, which is new for slugcats. Their throwing skill and spear damage is also higher. Spearmaster is also lighter: this gives the more stealth, and ability to fly if backflipping with squidcadas, however it also leaves them more susceptible to other issues, like wormgrass and predators.

Special mechanics:

Needles:

Spearmaster can create Needles from their tail at no cost by holding Grab with an empty hand. Pressing other inputs pauses Needle fabrication; a Needle can be created partway, paused indefinitely, and resumed later as long as Grab is held. Needles can be made while standing, crouching, in tunnels, on poles, and while airborne. They cannot be created while fully submerged. If Spearmaster is holding a creature or object that requires 2 hands, Needles can still be created but immediately detach and fall to the ground. Other than that, Needles function identically to Spears. They are just as valuable to Scavengers, and have the same uses as regular Spears.

Eating:

Spearmaster must siphon nutrients from creatures with their Needles in order to gain food pips. For a Needle to succeed in gaining food pips, it needs to be fresh. A Needle is considered fresh if it has not been previously thrown, put down, or otherwise knocked out of Spearmaster's hands. Inactive Needles gradually fade to black to signify this change. A Needle must hit and embed in a creature in order for food to be gained. Additionally, creatures only provide food while they are alive, meaning that corpses cannot be eaten from. As a fresh Needle is thrown, a long, organic strand is seen connecting the Needle to Spearmaster, acting as an intake of nutrients should a live and viable target be impaled successfully.

Backstory:

Spearmaster was created by the Iterator Seven Red Suns as a means of communication: a messenger if you will. Specifically, during their campaign they are sent to Five Pebbles to deliver a gold pearl: a pearl that will contain information on how to circumvent the self-destruction taboo. Spearmaster doesn't have the Mark of communication as Seven Red Suns said it is one sided, and would catch overseer attention: and opted instead to use a facial expression language. This campaign also appears to be earlier, as Looks to The Moon's structure is still intact.

Campaign goals/winning:

As always the ascension ending is open to all slugcats, including this one. However, they too have a specific campaign goal. You are, after all, a messenger sent to deliver your message. You will travel between Looks to The Moon and Five Pebbles, and then back again to encode different messages on different pearls. Once you obtain your final encoded pearl, you must then travel to the Communication Array in Sky Islands. This journey, although difficult, is rewarding. You obtain a lot of dialogue and lore reveals, and it ends the game with an adorable snapshot of Spearmaster, who has made its way safely back to Seven Red Suns, napping peacefully beside its owner.
