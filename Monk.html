*☆ Slugcat ☆*

♡ The Friendly slugcat ♡

Overview:

Monk is part of the base game with survivor and hunter. They serve as the game's easy mode. They have a strong spirit, and a good reputation!

Speed - strength - food

Monk is a weaker and much friendlier slugcat. Same normal diet as Survivor, of plants and small animals. 3 pips are needed to hibernate, and 2 can be stored for the next cycle.

Special mechanics:

Monk may be physically weaker, but they serve as easy mode for a reason. Despite the lesser damage, their reputation more than makes up for it. The game has a reputation system for creatures aggression- scavengers but also other creatures. This being said, Monk has a better reputation then the other slugcats, and their playthrough involves far less aggression and fighting.

Backstory:

Monk's opening cutscene is very similar to Survivor, however they did not fall off the log: they went after to save their family. They are shown to be a family with survivor, smaller and presumably younger. Their journey and campaign is to find their brother.

Campaign goals/winning:

The point of the campaign is to search for their brother: there are two endings. The traditional ascension ending, as explained on survivors page to end the endless cycles is the intended ending. However, there is a good ending for monk and survivor both! After completing the outer expanse ending in gourmand in the DLC, you unlock the outer expanse region for monk as well. Upon traveling here, monk will find Survivor waiting under the branch and reunite with them, living out a content life together! (this made me tear up…)
