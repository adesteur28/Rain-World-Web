*☆ Slugcat ☆*

♡ The Explosive slugcat ♡

Overview:

Artificer is strong, agile, and explosive. Literally. They can explode. This is their main mechanic, and appeal in the game. This campaign is in frequent conflict with the smartest AI in the game: scavengers. This path includes mandatory bloodshed to progress, and is full of conflict. It is unlocked after playing either survivor or monk of the base game.

Speed - strength - food

Another carnivorous creature. 6 pips are required to hibernate, and 3 are available to be stored. Again, fruits and plants only fill ¼ of a pip.They are immune to damage of explosives- even if they get stunned by them. Stats are very similar to hunter- 2x throw skill, and more strength, speed, etc. However, if under water too long they will explode and instant die, no drowning.

Special mechanics:

Bomb Jump:

Pressing either SPECIAL or both JUMP and GRAB while airborne causes Artificer to launch in the held direction. The amount of vertical momentum depends on how long the Jump input is held. Once a bomb jump is performed, another cannot be performed until Artificer makes contact with a floor or wall. This gives them a double jump basically, which makes traversing, fighting, escaping, and more easier. This is very necessary due to the difficulty of this campaign. However, if used too often artificer can overheat and hurt themselves.

Concussive Blast:

This is an offensive and defensive technique Artificer has. If DOWN is held while initiating a bomb jump, instead a concussive blast is produced. Artificer create a large explosion around themself, stunning nearby creatures and disarming scavengers. This also deflects projectiles from reaching artificer. The stun time is necessary both defensively and offensively. However, if used too often artificer can overheat and hurt themselves.

Mauling:

Pressing and holding GRAB with any living creature allows Artifcer to maul them. IF they are low enough health, or if it is a small creature, as mauling does one damage. Therefore, artificer doesn't need to eat just corpses.

Karma and scavengers:

Artificer cannot gain karma. Their karma is locked at 1, permanently. This is inconvenient, as you cannot make it through gates. The only way to get through gates is to slay and take the corpses of scavengers. Unfortunately, this and the already low scavenger reputation means that scavengers are unfriendable. They will send organized kill squads after artificer, which is part of what makes this campaign that much harder. Their organization, communication, accuracy, etc matches that of slugcats all too close, and the even match is difficult to manage.

Backstory:

Artificer is an interesting character because they have dreams to explain their past and their campaign, which other slugcats don't really have. In these dreams, its shown that Artificer is actually a mother to two slugpups- or was. Both of her children's deaths were caused by scavengers, leading to her vengeance. Artificer is a ruthless mother, who swears to take revenge and avenge her children's deaths.

Campaign goals/winning:

There are two endings. The ascension ending, a classic, has a beautiful artwork of artificer meeting her slugpups again in the void. The alternate ending is her revenge. The chieftain ending. By fighting your way to the top, you can beat the scavenger chieftain after talking to fivepebbles. By winning, you can wear their mask and unlock the ending "closure". So you get to choose: ascension and acceptance, or closure and revenge?
